## Configuration

All integration settings are managed through the **[Mesrai Dashboard](https://app.mesrai.com)**:

1. Go to **[app.mesrai.com](https://app.mesrai.com)**
2. Navigate to **Settings → Integrations**
3. Connect and configure your repositories
