Three plans. Two price modes per paid tier: **AI Included** (Mesrai-managed model) or **BYOK** (bring your own LLM key, lower platform fee). New accounts start on a 14-day Free Trial — every Pro feature unlocked, no card required.

<Note>**Currency** — Prices below are shown in both INR and USD. Indian customers are billed in INR (GST invoice issued). International customers are billed in USD. The price your account is charged is decided at checkout based on your billing country. Visit [mesrai.com/pricing](https://mesrai.com/pricing) for the auto-detected price in your local currency.</Note>

## Plans at a glance

- **Free Trial** — ₹0 / $0 for 14 days. Every feature unlocked, 1,000 AI credits provided, no credit card. Unlimited developers, repos, rules, and plugins during the trial.
- **Pro** — ₹999 / $12 per dev / mo (AI Included, 6,000 AI credits / dev / mo) **or** ₹499 / $6 per dev / mo (BYOK, unlimited PRs). Cockpit + Business Logic Validation + priority queue.
- **Enterprise** — custom. Custom AI allowance or unlimited BYOK. Self-hosted, SSO/SAML, Indian data residency, custom SLA.

All plans: unlimited repositories, all VCS providers, Mesrai Learnings.

<Note>1 AI credit = 1,000 tokens of model spend on Mesrai's managed AI. Typical PR reviews consume anywhere from a handful of credits up to a few hundred depending on diff size.</Note>

## AI Included vs BYOK

Two ways to power reviews. Pick per paid tier:

- **AI Included** — Mesrai supplies the LLM. One invoice covers seats + tokens. Pro is capped by a monthly AI-credit allowance (6,000 credits / dev / mo); Enterprise allowance is sized per contract. Pro tier exposes 3 model classes (Fast / Balanced / Premium); Enterprise unlocks all models plus custom endpoints.
- **BYOK** — Bring your own OpenAI, Anthropic, Vertex, Bedrock, or any OpenAI-compatible key. Lower platform fee. Token bill lands on your provider. Unlimited PR reviews. Switch providers anytime.

## How seats work

Mesrai bills per **active developer** — anyone who opens a PR in the billing period. Non-coding members never count. Resize freely in settings — no minimum, no annual seat lock-in. The 14-day Free Trial has no seat cap — invite the whole team.

## Free Trial — how it works

Every new account starts with a 14-day Free Trial. Every Pro feature is unlocked from day one, with 1,000 AI credits provided so you can run real PR reviews on real repos. No credit card required to start.

When the trial ends — or when the 1,000 credits run out, whichever comes first — reviews pause until you either upgrade to Pro or configure a BYOK key under **Organization → BYOK**. Your settings, integrations, rules, and history are preserved.

## Pro plan — what each price covers

**Pro AI Included (₹999 / $12 per dev / mo)** — Mesrai supplies the model. 6,000 AI credits per developer per month. 3 model classes: Fast (cheap, style + lint), Balanced (default), Premium (frontier for security and complex logic). When a developer's monthly credits run out, switch to BYOK to keep reviewing on your own AI key or wait for the next billing cycle.

**Pro BYOK (₹499 / $6 per dev / mo)** — Platform fee only. You connect your own LLM provider, pay your provider directly, no token markup from Mesrai. Unlimited reviews.

Both modes include over the trial baseline:

- Ongoing reviews past the 14-day trial window
- **Priority review queue** — Pro PRs reviewed ahead of trial traffic
- **GST invoice** for INR billing in India, tax-compliant invoice for USD billing internationally
- Email support, IST, 1 business day response

## Enterprise

Enterprise is contract-priced. Same two modes:

- **AI Included** — Mesrai-managed model, custom AI allowance sized to your team, full model catalog plus custom endpoints.
- **BYOK** — bring your own provider, unlimited reviews.

Enterprise-only:

- **Self-hosted deployment** in your VPC
- **SSO / SAML**
- **RBAC + audit logs**
- **Indian data residency**
- **Custom SLA**
- **5 dedicated support hours / month**
- **Onboarding session**
- **TDS documentation**

## Payments and billing

- **India** — INR billing on Pro and Enterprise. GST invoice issued every transaction.
- **International** — USD billing on Pro and Enterprise. Tax-compliant invoice issued every transaction.
- Currency is set at checkout based on your billing country and remains fixed for the lifetime of the subscription. Cancel and re-subscribe to change currency.
- Enterprise includes TDS documentation on request.
- Cancel anytime — change applies at end of current cycle.
