Three plans. Two price modes per tier: **AI Included** (Mesrai-managed model) or **BYOK** (bring your own LLM key, lower platform fee).

## Plans at a glance

- **Free** — ₹0. 3 active devs max. 25 AI Included PRs / month (org-wide) or **unlimited** BYOK reviews.
- **Pro** — ₹999 / dev / mo (AI Included, 150 PR / dev / mo) **or** ₹499 / dev / mo (BYOK, unlimited PRs). Cockpit + Business Logic Validation + priority queue.
- **Enterprise** — custom. 300 PR / dev / mo AI Included or unlimited BYOK. Self-hosted, SSO/SAML, Indian data residency, custom SLA.

All plans: unlimited repositories, all VCS providers, Mesrai Learnings.

## AI Included vs BYOK

Two ways to power reviews. Pick per tier:

- **AI Included** — Mesrai supplies the LLM. One invoice covers seats + tokens. Capped by PR / dev / mo (25 org / 150 dev / 300 dev). Pro tier exposes 3 model classes (Fast / Balanced / Premium); Enterprise unlocks all models plus custom endpoints.
- **BYOK** — Bring your own OpenAI, Anthropic, Vertex, Bedrock, or any OpenAI-compatible key. Lower platform fee. Token bill lands on your provider. Unlimited PR reviews. Switch providers anytime.

| Mode | Free | Pro | Enterprise |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **AI Included** | ₹0 (25 PR org/mo) | ₹999 / dev / mo (150 PR/dev/mo) | Custom (300 PR/dev/mo) |
| **BYOK** | ₹0 (unlimited) | ₹499 / dev / mo (unlimited) | Custom (unlimited) |

See the [BYOK guide](/guides/byok) for supported providers and setup.

## How seats work

Mesrai bills per **active developer** — anyone who opens a PR in the billing period. Non-coding members never count. Resize freely in settings — no minimum, no annual seat lock-in. Free caps at 3 active devs / org.

## Pro plan — what each price covers

**Pro AI Included (₹999 / dev / mo)** — Mesrai supplies the model. 150 reviews per dev per month. 3 model classes: Fast (cheap, style + lint), Balanced (default), Premium (frontier for security and complex logic).

**Pro BYOK (₹499 / dev / mo)** — Platform fee only. You connect your own LLM provider, pay your provider directly, no token markup from Mesrai. Unlimited reviews.

Both modes include over Free:

- Unlimited Mesrai Playbook (Free cap of 10 lifted)
- Unlimited plugins / MCPs (Free cap of 3 lifted)
- Unlimited active developers (Free cap of 3 lifted)
- **Priority review queue** — Pro PRs reviewed ahead of Free traffic
- **Mesrai Cockpit** — engineering metrics dashboard
- **Business Logic Validation**
- **GST invoice** and **INR billing**
- Email support, IST, 1 business day response

## Enterprise

Enterprise is contract-priced. Same two modes:

- **AI Included** — Mesrai-managed model, 300 PR / dev / mo, full model catalog plus custom endpoints.
- **BYOK** — bring your own provider, unlimited reviews.

Enterprise-only:

- **Self-hosted deployment** in your VPC
- **SSO / SAML**
- **RBAC + audit logs**
- **Indian data residency**
- **Custom SLA**
- **5 dedicated support hours / month**
- **Onboarding session**
- **TDS documentation**
