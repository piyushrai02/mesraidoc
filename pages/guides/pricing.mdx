## How BYOK works

Mesrai runs reviews on **your own LLM key** — bring OpenAI, Anthropic, Vertex, Bedrock, or any OpenAI-compatible provider. You pay the provider directly at their rates; Mesrai never marks up tokens and charges only a flat per-developer platform fee. Reviews are unlimited on every tier, and you can switch providers anytime.

| Tier | Free Trial | Pro | Enterprise |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **BYOK** | ₹0 / $0 (unlimited, 14 days) | ₹499 / $6 per dev / mo (unlimited) | Custom (unlimited) |

See the [BYOK guide](/guides/byok) for supported providers and setup.

## How seats work

Mesrai bills per **active developer** — anyone who opens a PR in the billing period. Non-coding members never count. Resize freely in settings — no minimum, no annual seat lock-in. The 14-day Free Trial has no seat cap — invite the whole team.

## Free Trial — how it works

Every new account starts with a 14-day Free Trial. Every Pro feature is unlocked from day one. Connect your own AI key under **Organization → BYOK** to run real PR reviews on real repos. No credit card required to start.

When the trial ends, reviews continue on Pro (₹499 / $6 per dev / mo) as long as a BYOK key is configured. Your settings, integrations, rules, and history are preserved.

## Pro plan — what it covers

**Pro (₹499 / $6 per dev / mo)** — Platform fee only. You connect your own LLM provider and pay them directly, with no token markup from Mesrai. Unlimited reviews, plus:

- **3 model classes** — Fast (cheap, style + lint), Balanced (default), Premium (frontier for security and complex logic)
- **Priority review queue** — Pro PRs reviewed ahead of trial traffic
- **GST invoice** for INR billing in India, tax-compliant invoice for USD billing internationally
- Email support, IST, 1 business day response
