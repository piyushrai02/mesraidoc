## Languages

Mesrai reviews any language or file type. Semantic understanding from the LLM; repo-wide context from a code-search index that resolves identifiers across files.

## Why teams pick Mesrai

- **Reviews ship fast** — automate the routine, free seniors for the hard calls.
- **Standards stay enforced** — rules you wrote, applied to every PR consistently.
- **The system learns** — memories + auto-generated rules grow with your codebase.
