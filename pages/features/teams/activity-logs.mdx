# Activity Logs

<DevelopmentNote />

Track every action within your team with detailed audit logs.

## What are Activity Logs?

Activity logs record every action taken within your team, providing a complete history for:
- Security auditing
- Compliance requirements
- Team management
- Troubleshooting issues

## Logged Actions

### Team Management
- Team creation
- Team name changes
- Team deletion
- Settings updates

### Member Actions
- Member additions
- Member removals
- Role changes (Owner, Admin, Member)
- Permission modifications

### Repository Actions
- Repository connections
- Repository disconnections
- Access level changes

### Invitation Management
- Invitations sent
- Invitations accepted
- Invitations declined
- Invitations revoked

## Viewing Activity Logs

1. Navigate to your team page
2. Click the "Activity" tab
3. View chronological list of actions
4. Filter by action type, date, or member

## Log Entry Format

Each log entry contains:
- **Timestamp**: When the action occurred
- **Actor**: Who performed the action
- **Action Type**: What was done
- **Target**: What was affected
- **Details**: Additional context

Example:
```
2025-01-15 10:30:45 UTC
Actor: john@example.com
Action: MEMBER_ADDED
Target: jane@example.com
Role: Admin
```

## Filtering Logs

Use filters to find specific activities:

### By Action Type
- Member management
- Repository management
- Team settings
- Invitations

### By Date Range
- Last 24 hours
- Last 7 days
- Last 30 days
- Custom range

### By Team Member
- View actions by specific users
- Track individual contributions

## Exporting Logs

Export activity logs for compliance or analysis:

1. Click "Export" button
2. Select date range
3. Choose format (CSV, JSON, PDF)
4. Download report

## Retention Policy

Activity logs are retained based on your plan:
- **Starter**: 30 days
- **Pro**: 90 days
- **Team**: 1 year
- **Enterprise**: Unlimited
