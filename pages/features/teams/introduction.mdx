# Teams Overview

<DevelopmentNote />

Mesrai Teams enables collaborative code review workflows across your engineering organization.

## What are Teams?

Teams are groups of developers who collaborate on specific repositories. Each team can have:
- Multiple team members with different roles
- Connected repositories with granular permissions
- Shared analytics and activity tracking
- Custom notification settings

## Key Features

### Team Analytics
Track your team's performance with real-time metrics:
- **Member Count**: Total active team members
- **Repository Count**: Connected repositories
- **PRs Reviewed**: Total pull requests analyzed
- **Comments Generated**: AI-generated review comments
- **Last Activity**: Most recent team action

[Learn more about Team Analytics →](/features/teams/team-analytics)

### Activity Logs
Complete audit trail of all team actions:
- Team creation and updates
- Member additions and removals
- Role changes
- Repository connections
- Invitation management

[Learn more about Activity Logs →](/features/teams/activity-logs)

### Team Invitations
Invite colleagues via email with role-based access:
- **Owner**: Full team management
- **Admin**: Member and repository management
- **Member**: Read and review access

[Learn more about Invitations →](/features/teams/invitations)

## Getting Started

1. Navigate to the Teams page in your dashboard
2. Click "Create Team"
3. Add team members and connect repositories
4. Configure team settings and permissions
